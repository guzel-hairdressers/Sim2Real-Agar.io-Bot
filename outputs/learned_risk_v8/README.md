Eight-second, second-generation on-policy corrective training. Physical split limits are mass, piece capacity and cooldown only. Movement assistance remains active. Both split heads were fitted for 240 epochs, selected with calibrated development utility; movement/value/critic weights are unchanged.

Frozen before fresh final evaluation. Development PPO retained 135.0% of baseline mean final mass with equal full deaths; diffusion retained 89.5% with zero deaths versus one. These six-match development results do not establish acceptance. There is no deployment until the declared fresh encounters, twelve retention matches per policy and the twelve-match current-policy league pass and the promotion script writes a verified manifest.
